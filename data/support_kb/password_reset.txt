Title: Helping Customers Reset Their Password

If a customer cannot log in and requests a password reset, direct them to
the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. This sends a reset email valid
for 30 minutes. If they do not receive the email, check that it was not
filtered to spam and confirm the email address on file matches what they
are using to log in. If the account uses SSO (Google or Microsoft login),
password resets do not apply; direct them to reset their password with
their identity provider instead.
