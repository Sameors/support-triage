Title: Processing Account Cancellation Requests

When a customer requests to cancel their account, confirm whether they want
immediate cancellation or cancellation at the end of the current billing
cycle. Immediate cancellation forfeits any remaining prepaid time; end-of-cycle
cancellation retains access until the cycle ends with no further charges.
Ask for a brief reason for cancellation if the customer is willing to share
one, for internal tracking, but do not require it. Send a confirmation email
once cancellation is processed.
