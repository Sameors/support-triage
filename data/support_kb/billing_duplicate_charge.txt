Title: Resolving Duplicate Charges on an Invoice

If a customer reports being charged twice for the same invoice, first confirm
the duplicate by checking the payment processor logs for two transactions
with matching invoice numbers and amounts within a short time window.

Common causes: a retried failed payment that actually succeeded both times,
a browser double-submit on the checkout page, or a manual re-entry by support
staff. Once confirmed, refund the duplicate transaction (not the original)
within 3-5 business days. Notify the customer with the refund confirmation
and expected timeline. If the duplicate cannot be confirmed from logs alone,
escalate to the billing team for manual transaction reconciliation.
