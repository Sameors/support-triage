Title: Handling Shipping Delay Complaints

If a customer reports their order has not arrived by the estimated delivery
date, first check the tracking number for the latest carrier update. Delays
of 1-3 days beyond the estimate are common and do not require compensation.
For delays beyond 7 days with no tracking movement, offer a partial shipping
refund or a small account credit, per policy. If tracking shows the package
as delivered but the customer disputes this, open a carrier investigation
and offer a replacement or refund once the investigation confirms non-delivery.
